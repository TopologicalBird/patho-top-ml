## Trustworthiness for multiple neighborhood sizes
![trustworthiness.pdf](trustworthiness.pdf)

Visualization of trustworthiness values for multiple neighborhood sizes with different frameworks. U+T, U+C, and full have higher trustworthiness compared to other frameworks. When comparing U+T and U+C, in smaller neighborhood sizes, U+C performs better, and in larger neighborhood sizes, U+T performs better. This change takes place around $k=50$--$100$.
